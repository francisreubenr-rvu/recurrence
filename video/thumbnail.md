# Thumbnail

16:9, 1280×720 or larger. The guide suggests Google's Nano Banana (Gemini image), with a photo of one or more team members attached. Use your own photos only, with everyone's consent.

## Concept

Split screen. **Left**, dim and red-tinted: a phone lock screen showing a page alert, "payments-api 5xx 19%, 7:47 PM", next to a generic chatbot bubble saying "Restart the pods". **Right**, bright: a team member looking at a laptop, with a green-outlined card that reads "Seen this 25 days ago. Do NOT restart." A thin "memory" line connects a small stack of incident cards (INC-2041, INC-2087) to the right side.

## Text on the thumbnail (big, 3–5 words; pick one)

1. **IT REMEMBERED THE OUTAGE**
2. **"SEEN THIS 25 DAYS AGO"**
3. **STOP REPEATING BAD FIXES**

Small corner label: `Hindsight memory`. No event name, no other logos unless you have the right to use them.

## Prompt to paste into Nano Banana (attach the team photo)

```
Create a 16:9 YouTube thumbnail, high contrast and readable on a phone.
Left third: dark red-tinted phone lock screen with a pager alert "payments-api 5xx 19% · 7:47 PM" and a grey chat bubble "Restart the pods".
Right two thirds: the person from the attached photo, lit warmly, looking at a laptop, with a bold green-outlined card floating beside them reading "Seen this 25 days ago. Do NOT restart."
A glowing thin line links a small stack of cards labelled INC-2041 and INC-2087 to the green card.
Huge headline text across the top: "IT REMEMBERED THE OUTAGE".
Small label bottom-right: "Hindsight memory".
Clean, modern, dark background, no clutter, no extra text, no watermarks.
```

Check the output: spelling of the text (image models often garble it; fix it in Canva if needed), faces look like the real people, readable at 320 px wide.
